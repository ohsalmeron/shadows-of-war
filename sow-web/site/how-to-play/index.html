01 · First deployment

Five moves
to understand
the match.

01

Join matchmaking

Open Play Now and enter the current matchmaking lobby. The web build starts in your browser without an account or a separate download.

02

Choose your commander

Select one of the current leaders. Their command trait changes a strategic edge such as armies, expansion, production, defense, troop capacity, or income.

03

Expand and build

Claim connected territory, grow your border, and construct structures. Cities, factories, ports, armories, and defense towers turn land into leverage.

04

Build the economy

Gold and troop income create momentum. Protect your production, use ports for naval support, and attack when the cost is worth the ground gained.

05

Ally, betray, control

Form alliances, coordinate resources, and break an alliance when the front line demands it. In the default FFA rules, controlling 60% of the land or eliminating the opposition ends the match.

Match structure

The server keeps one rolling matchmaking lobby and rotates the mode and map selection over time.

  • FFA
  • Teams
  • Humans vs Nations
  • Weighted world-map rotation
