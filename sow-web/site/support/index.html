Direct Email Support

For connection issues, account problems, match state glitches, or general inquiries, contact us directly at hello@shadowsofwar.io.

Please include your browser version, device, approximate match timestamp, and a summary of what occurred.

05

Security & Vulnerability Reporting

We take security seriously. Please do not post security vulnerabilities in public forums or public GitHub issues. Send vulnerability reports privately to hello@shadowsofwar.io. We acknowledge reports within 48 hours.

06

Privacy & Data Deletion

To access, correct, or delete your data, email hello@shadowsofwar.io with your in-game account ID (or display name plus approximate creation date). Verified deletions permanently erase the account, its platform-identity mappings, and analytics memberships.
