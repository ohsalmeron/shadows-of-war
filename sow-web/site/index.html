04 · Game mechanics

Know the
rules.

Key information on gameplay mechanics, tactical systems, and matchmaking.

What is Shadows of War?

A match-based real-time strategy game where players choose a leader, expand territory, build an economy, form alliances, and fight for control of the world map.

What does MMORTS mean here?

Massively multiplayer online real-time strategy: map-based lobbies with high player capacity, live expansion, diplomacy, structures, and combat.

Do I need an account or a download?

No. You can start playing immediately in any modern browser without mandatory registration or downloads.

What makes the leaders different?

Each leader has a unique command trait that changes strategic advantages: armies, expansion, production, defense, troop capacity, or gold generation.

Can I form alliances and betray rivals?

Yes. Alliances support coordination and resource interaction; betrayal is an explicit tactical decision that shifts the front line immediately.

How does matchmaking work?

The server maintains a rolling matchmaking lobby that rotates between Free-For-All (FFA), Teams, and Humans vs Nations on active maps.

Where can I play right now?

The official live build is at shadowsofwar.io across desktop and mobile browsers.

Is the project open source?

Yes. The client and server source code are available on GitHub.
