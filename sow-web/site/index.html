Game systems

How matches
work.

Rules that shape every match.

How do I start a match?

Open the game, choose a leader, and join an active lobby.

What happens during a match?

Expand territory, build cities, factories, ports, and bunkers, form alliances, attack, and contest map control.

Which game modes are available?

The matchmaking queue rotates through Free-For-All, Teams, and Humans vs Nations on active maps.

Do I need an account?

No. You can join a match without registering.

What do leaders change?

Each leader changes a core advantage such as combat strength, expansion speed, gold generation, troop income, or garrison capacity.
