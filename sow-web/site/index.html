About Shadows of War

Shadows of War is a free-to-play, open-source massive multiplayer online real-time strategy game. Command civilizations on world maps, form alliances with other players, expand your territory, and manage economy while fighting large-scale battles online.

Single-player works offline; multiplayer runs on our public server. Play on the web or native desktop and mobile clients.
