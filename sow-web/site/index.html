04 · Field questions

Know the
rules.

The short version of the current web build. For the actual opening moves, read the field manual.

What is Shadows of War?

A match-based browser MMORTS where players choose a leader, expand territory, build an economy, form alliances, and fight for control of the map in real time.

What does MMORTS mean here?

Massively multiplayer online real-time strategy: map-based lobbies with large player capacity, live expansion, diplomacy, structures, and combat.

Do I need an account or a download?

No. The current web build starts in the browser without an account or a separate download.

What makes the leaders different?

Each current leader has a command trait that changes a strategic edge: armies, expansion, production, defense, troop capacity, or income.

Can I form alliances and betray rivals?

Yes. Alliances support coordination and resource interaction; betrayal is an explicit decision that can change the front line immediately.

How does matchmaking work?

The server keeps a rolling matchmaking lobby and rotates between FFA, Teams, and Humans vs Nations with a weighted map rotation.

Where can I play right now?

The official live build is at shadowsofwar.io. Other platform listings are not live yet.

Is the project open source?

Yes. The source and attribution information are available on GitHub.
