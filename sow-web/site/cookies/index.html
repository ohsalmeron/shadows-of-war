01

What we use

Shadows of War uses strictly-necessary client storage only — the kind that needs no consent banner because the site and game cannot function without it:

  • Theme preference (sow-theme, localStorage): remembers your light/dark choice. First-party, strictly necessary.
  • Analytics session identifiers (sow_site_session_id, sow_analytics_session, sessionStorage): random IDs that group first-party product events within one visit. They vanish when the tab closes.
  • First-party product events: anonymous funnel events sent to our own servers (/api/event). Not a cookie, not shared with any vendor.
  • Game settings & cached map assets (browser local storage): client preferences and downloaded game data.
02

Third parties

Font files are fetched from Google Fonts, which applies Google's own policies to that fetch. No other third-party code runs storage or tracking on our pages: no advertising networks, no social widgets, no session-replay scripts, no cross-site identifiers.

03

What we don't do

  • No advertising cookies and no interest-based advertising.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data for advertising.
  • No cross-site or cross-app tracking.

Because there is no behavioral tracking, Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals have nothing to switch off — and we honor GPC as a sale/sharing opt-out regardless.

04

Your controls

  • Clear site data anytime in your browser settings — this removes the theme preference, session IDs, and cached assets. The game keeps working; it simply forgets your theme and starts a fresh session.
  • Blocking all storage may force re-downloads of game assets and reset preferences each visit.
  • For personal-data rights (access, deletion, portability), see the Privacy Policy — email hello@shadowsofwar.io.
05

Changes

If we ever add non-essential storage (analytics vendors, embeds, advertising), this notice will list each item with its purpose and controls before it ships — and the Privacy Policy will be updated alongside. Last updated: September 2026.
