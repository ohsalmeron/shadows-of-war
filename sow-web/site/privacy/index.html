Privacy
policy.

Last updated: August 2026

01

Overview

Shadows of War is a multiplayer strategy game. This policy describes what data we process when you play on shadowsofwar.io. If the game is later published through a partner platform, that platform may apply its own privacy policy and SDK rules.

02

Data We Collect

  • Gameplay data: Display name, clan tag, leader choice, and match inputs required for multiplayer synchronization.
  • Technical data: Client build version, WebSocket connection metadata, and basic server logs for abuse prevention.
  • Anonymous product events: Page visits, boot stages, tutorial progress, matchmaking, match completion, and session timing. We use these events for activation, retention, and reliability analysis, retain them for up to 90 days, and do not sell them.
  • Partner platforms: If published through a partner platform, its SDK may collect analytics under its own policy.
03

Local Storage

The client uses browser local storage exclusively for settings and cached map assets. No third-party advertising trackers are embedded in the client.

04

Source Code & Inquiries
