Overview

Shadows of War is a browser and native multiplayer strategy game. This policy describes what data we process when you play on shadowsofwar.io. If the game is later published through a partner platform, that platform may apply its own privacy policy and SDK rules.

Data we collect

  • Gameplay data such as display name, clan tag, leader choice, and match inputs required for multiplayer.
  • Technical data such as client build version, WebSocket connection metadata, and basic server logs for abuse prevention.
  • If the game is later published through a partner platform, its SDK may collect analytics under its own policy. We do not sell personal data.

Local storage

The web client may use browser local storage for settings and cached map assets. No third-party advertising trackers are embedded in the open-source client.

Source and contact

Server-side components are licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The corresponding source is available on GitHub. Privacy and security inquiries can be sent to info@shadowsofwar.io.
