Overview

Shadows of War is a multiplayer strategy game. This policy describes what data we process when you play on shadowsofwar.io. If the game is later published through a partner platform, that platform may apply its own privacy policy and SDK rules.

Data we collect

  • Gameplay data such as display name, clan tag, leader choice, and match inputs required for multiplayer.
  • Technical data such as client build version, WebSocket connection metadata, and basic server logs for abuse prevention.
  • Anonymous product events such as page visits, boot stages, tutorial progress, matchmaking, match completion, and gameplay session timing. We use these events for activation, retention, and reliability analysis, retain them for up to 90 days, and do not sell them.
  • If the game is later published through a partner platform, its SDK may collect analytics under its own policy.

Local storage

The client may use browser local storage for settings and cached map assets. No third-party advertising trackers are embedded in the client.

Source and contact

Server-side components are licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The corresponding source is available on GitHub. Privacy and security inquiries can be sent to info@shadowsofwar.io.
