### 17. Pacal the Great | Maya Civilization | 603–683 CE
- **Hero:** Athletic, jade mosaic death mask worn pushed back on the head, intricate jade pectorals and loincloth, holding a stylized double-headed serpent bar. Mystical, deeply spiritual, commanding the cosmos.
- **Empire:** Palenque Temple of the Inscriptions, towering chunky step pyramids emerging from dense jungle canopy, stylized ceiba trees, steamy tropical mist, jade and limestone palette.
